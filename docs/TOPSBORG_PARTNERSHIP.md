# TOPSBORG Technologies Limited Partnership

## Approved public positioning

TOPSBORG Technologies Limited is SESC's Official Technology Partner and technology implementation partner under the Goods/Services Sponsorship Agreement. TOPSBORG designs and develops the SESC digital platform.

The verified public destination is [topsborgtech.com](https://topsborgtech.com). Keep SESC as the primary brand and community authority in every placement. This partnership does not state or imply that TOPSBORG owns, operates, controls, or represents SESC.

## Canonical source and safe external links

The partnership name, role, agreement and website are maintained in src/config/site.ts. Public components must use the shared TopsborgWebsiteLink component instead of duplicating a TOPSBORG website URL.

Every outbound TOPSBORG website link is a semantic anchor with target=_blank and rel=sponsored noopener noreferrer. The sponsored qualifier accurately signals the partnership relationship, while noopener and noreferrer protect the opened tab relationship. Link text or its accessible name must make clear that it opens TOPSBORG's official website.

## Change procedure

1. Update src/config/site.ts only after the official name, role, agreement or website has been verified.
2. Keep new public website placements on TopsborgWebsiteLink so the URL, sponsorship qualifier and tab-security attributes remain consistent.
3. Keep the acknowledgment secondary to SESC and avoid promotional animation, pop-ups, repeated badges or intrusive logo treatment.
4. Run the unit and browser checks, including the documented mobile, tablet and desktop viewports, before publishing.
5. Obtain authorised SESC and TOPSBORG review whenever public facts, assets or partnership claims change.
