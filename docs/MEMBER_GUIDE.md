# Member Guide

## Preview status

The current membership journey is a development preview. It deliberately does not render a data-entry form and its endpoint rejects requests before reading personal information. It does **not** currently create a Supabase account, store an application, receive an identity document or receipt, process a bank transfer, activate a membership, issue a live card, send email, or verify a real membership.

Do not submit real personal information, identification documents, payment evidence, or bank details to this preview until SESC confirms that the live service is available.

## Intended membership journey

When the secure production service is enabled, the expected experience is:

1. Create or sign in to a verified account.
2. Complete personal, contact, chapter, membership-category, emergency-contact, and declaration details.
3. Save a draft and return later where the authenticated service supports it.
4. Provide only the requested profile photo and identity evidence through a protected private upload route.
5. Follow official manual bank-transfer instructions supplied by SESC; never send card details through the form.
6. Submit the required payment reference/receipt through the private workflow.
7. Track the application's status and respond to a correction request if one is issued.
8. Wait for authorised finance and membership review before treating the membership as active.
9. Receive a digital card only after activation and use its secure verification link when requested.

An application or payment submission is not membership activation. Eligibility, category terms, fees, payment instructions, and membership duration must be confirmed by SESC before launch.

## Protect your information

- Use a unique password and do not share your account with anyone, including a club volunteer.
- Upload only requested documents through the official HTTPS SESC platform after it is live.
- Never send passwords, card details, OTPs, or identity documents by public social media, chat, or email.
- Verify payment instructions through an approved SESC contact before transferring money. The current project does not publish official bank instructions.
- Check the website address before signing in or entering a payment reference.
- Report a suspicious message, incorrect account access, or lost device through the club's approved support route when one is published.

## Digital membership card and public verification

The planned digital card includes an approved name, membership number, chapter, category, issue/expiry dates, status, and a verification reference. The public verifier is designed to reveal only safe active-card information. It must never reveal your address, phone number, email address, payment history, emergency contact, identity document, or other sensitive data.

If a verifier requests more than basic card validation, do not provide private documents without contacting SESC through an approved channel.

## Account and communication preferences

The eventual member portal will provide profile controls, application/payment status, membership card, notifications, and marketing preferences. Necessary security and membership messages may still be sent when required to operate the service; marketing messages should use consent and a working unsubscribe option. See [Email operations](EMAIL.md).

## Accessibility and support

The interface is being built for keyboard, touch, and responsive use. If you encounter an accessibility barrier in the live service, record the page, device/browser, and what happened, then contact the approved accessibility/support route once it is published. Do not include unnecessary identity or payment data in a support request.

## Before relying on the platform

Wait for an official SESC launch announcement that supplies the live website address, contact route, privacy policy, membership terms, payment instructions, and support process. Until then, the pages and portals are a development demonstration rather than a membership service.
